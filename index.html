  • Mark a trail complete to silence it

03

03Camp

The journey continues without you.

Every expedition needs a camp. Trek pitches one on your Mac, and trek-server sets one up on any Mac, Windows or Linux machine. Close the lid and walk away; your agents keep going, and Trek picks up right where you left them. Every window and every phone on a project sees the same sessions, live.

  • No account, no relay, no telemetry. Camp is yours
  • One token per traveller's device, revoke any time
  • Reach camp over Tailscale, your LAN or wss://
  • Runs on macOS, Windows and Linux, as a background service

04Guides

Choose your guide for the terrain.

Different ground calls for different guides. Trek runs the real CLIs in a real terminal, not an SDK wrapper: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, Grok, or a local model through Ollama or LM Studio. Slash commands, skills, plugins, hooks and permission prompts all work the way they do in your own terminal, and your subscription is used the same way too.

  • Local models welcome, through Ollama or LM Studio
  • Save session types with their own guide and briefing
  • Your CLIs, your logins, your config

05Expedition lead

One guide to lead the expedition.

The Global panel (⌘J) is a project-wide agent with Trek's own MCP tools. It breaks new trails across your repositories, sends guides down them, queues their next legs, reads their progress and waits for them to report back. When the route forks, it asks you to decide.

  • Queue the next leg for a session to start when it's ready
  • Tag, note and link a trail from a prompt
  • Tools stay on 127.0.0.1, scoped to the project

06Trail log

Every trail keeps its log.

Mark how far each trail has come: In Progress, In Review, Ready to Deploy. Pin its Linear issue and pull request, and leave notes for whoever walks it next. The log belongs to the branch, so it's still there when you come back to it.

  • Linear issue status and title on the chip
  • Filter the map by how far along each trail is
  • All Notes gathers every trail's log in one place
