Privacy Policy

Trek runs on your machines. Your code, prompts, terminal output and agent sessions stay on computers you control, and we don't sell or share your data.

Effective November 17, 2026

This policy covers the Trek desktop app, trek-server, the Trek app for iOS and Android (together, "Trek"), the Trek push relay, and this website, gettrek.app. "We" and "us" means the developer of Trek.

The short version

  • Trek has no accounts and no sign-up. We don't know who you are.
  • Your projects, code, prompts, terminal output and agent sessions live on your computer, or a server you run yourself. They are never sent to us.
  • The apps contain no advertising, no analytics and no tracking SDKs.
  • If you turn on push notifications in the mobile app, we hold your device's push token so we can deliver them. Nothing else.
  • This website uses cookie-free, privacy-friendly analytics, and keeps your email address only if you sign up for the Android beta.

The desktop app and trek-server

Trek runs agent command-line tools (such as Claude Code, Codex, Gemini or Grok) and local models on your computer, or on a trek-server you install on a machine of your own. Your projects, worktrees, settings, session history and terminal output are stored on that machine. We do not operate those servers and cannot see anything on them.

The agent tools you choose to run are made by other companies and send your prompts and code to their providers under those providers' own terms and privacy policies. Trek uses the sign-ins you already have with them; it does not handle, store or forward those credentials to us. The same applies to services you connect, like GitHub or Linear.

Update checks. When it starts, and once a day after that, the desktop app asks GitHub for the latest Trek release. That request goes to GitHub, which sees your IP address as it would for any web request. Nothing about you or your projects is included.

The iOS and Android app

The mobile app is a remote control for your own Trek servers. It talks directly to the servers you pair it with, and we are not in the middle of that connection.

  • Paired servers. Each server's name, address and access token are stored only on your phone, in its secure storage (the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). Removing a server or deleting the app removes them.
  • Camera. Used only to scan a server's pairing QR code, and only when you open the scanner. No photos or video are saved or sent anywhere.
  • Local network. Used to reach a Trek server on your home or office network.
  • Your project data. Sessions, terminal output and worktrees you view in the app are loaded from your own server and are not sent to us.

Push notifications

Push notifications are optional and off until you turn them on. Apple and Google only let an app be woken through their push services (Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging), so the Trek push relay delivers them on behalf of your servers.

  • What the relay stores: your device's push token, whether it's an iOS or Android device, whether you've paused notifications, and a random grant for each server you allowed to notify you, with timestamps for each.
  • What a notification contains: only the kind of event (for example "a decision is waiting" or "pull request merged"), the project's name, and internal IDs so the app opens the right screen. Terminal text, questions from your agents, branch names and code are never sent.
  • What it never sees: the relay doesn't receive your server's address or credentials, and your servers never see your push token.
  • Logs: the relay logs the kind of each notification and whether it was delivered, never its words, tokens or secrets.
  • Deletion: turning notifications off, or removing a server, deletes the matching data from the relay. Devices that Apple or Google report as no longer reachable are deleted after 30 days.

Notifications are delivered by Apple or Google, under the Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

This website

  • Analytics. We use a self-hosted instance of Umami to count page views. It sets no cookies, doesn't track you across sites, and doesn't store your IP address. It records things like the page, referrer, browser, device type and country.
  • Android beta. If you sign up for the beta, we store your email address, the platform you chose and when you signed up. We use it only to invite you to test the app (by adding the address to the Google Play closed test) and to tell you when it's available, and delete it on request.
  • Downloads. To find the right installer, your browser asks GitHub for the latest release. Downloads are served by GitHub.
  • Fonts and hosting. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the site is served through Cloudflare. Like any web server, these see your IP address and browser details to deliver the page.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or trade personal data.
  • We don't use your data for advertising or to build profiles.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't use your code, prompts or sessions to train AI models. We never receive them.

Children

Trek is a developer tool and isn't directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live). We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Server tokens on your phone are kept in the platform's secure storage. The push relay's data and credentials are readable only by the service that runs it, and connections to it and to this website use HTTPS.

Your choices and rights

Since we hold very little, most of your data is under your control already: delete it from your own machines, remove a server from the mobile app, or turn off notifications. To ask what we hold about you, or to have your beta sign-up email or push data deleted, email us at the address below. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We'll honor those requests wherever you live.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the effective date at the top. If a change materially affects how we handle your data, we'll make it clear in the app or on this site.

Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@gettrek.app, or open an issue on GitHub.
